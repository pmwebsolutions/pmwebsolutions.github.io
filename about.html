The Story Behind the Pivot

Akusama wasn't born in a typical agency boardroom. It began with a realization from our founders—former Product Managers at global tech giants—that Kenyan SMEs were being underserved by "template-first" developers.

We saw a landscape of brilliant businesses held back by fragile digital infrastructure. Our rebranding from a boutique studio to a technical powerhouse was fueled by the desire to bring Silicon Valley-grade architectural precision to the vibrant markets of Nairobi, Mombasa, and beyond.

Today, we combine ex-PM strategic thinking with world-class engineering to build platforms that don't just look beautiful—they perform under pressure.
